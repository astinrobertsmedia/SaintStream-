Membership Growth

LDSPMA becomes visible throughout the creator journey, not just at conferences.

See more

SaintStream can give LDSPMA a meaningful presence throughout the creator account experience.

Rather than hiding LDSPMA behind a link, the app can invite creators to connect with LDSPMA at natural points in their development: joining the LDSPMA tribe, connecting an LDSPMA account, receiving support, earning recognition, participating in service opportunities, and finding mentors.

How LDSPMA appears in creator accounts
  • Contextual invitations to join the LDSPMA tribe.
  • LDSPMA member badges on creator profiles.
  • Recognition for ongoing service and creator contribution.
  • Mentorship and support pathways connected to LDSPMA membership.
  • Member spotlights, contests, awards, and conference opportunities.
The strategic role

LDSPMA can become the unifying organization for student recruitment and professional development, while SaintStream becomes the public-facing home of the student creator community.

2

Student Pipeline

Students collaborate, form teams, receive feedback, seek funding, and grow through daily development.

See more

Students do not simply “create inside SaintStream.” SaintStream becomes the ecosystem through which they collaborate, organize teams, receive feedback, interact with mentors, work with professors, complete milestones, build portfolios, and move projects toward completion.

Professors can use SaintStream as a practical project ecosystem where assignments contribute to meaningful outcomes instead of disappearing at the end of a semester.

Professor-supported student work
  • Students form tribe teams for team projects.
  • Professors assign students to post increments, drafts, checkpoints, and final projects in support and feedback channels.
  • LDSPMA members and mentors can review promising work.
  • Projects can serve the SaintStream community of children and families.
Why this matters

Instead of connecting with LDSPMA once a year through a conference, students receive daily development opportunities through collaboration, mentorship, project work, funding experience, and professional networking.

Students also gain real-world marketing experience by learning how to present projects, explain impact, build support, and invite funding from people who care about the mission.

A living portfolio for future opportunity

A student's SaintStream account becomes a living portfolio — every project, credit, collaboration, finished work, and audience response stays attached to their profile and grows with them.

That portfolio can support future employment, agency applications, grad-school submissions, LDSPMA opportunities, and independent creator income streams as they build an audience around work they care about.

Students leave school with more than a degree. They leave with a public body of work, a credible track record, and a community that already knows what they can do.

Community Between Conferences

LDSPMA tribes create year-round creator communities, contests, mentoring, and feedback.

See more

SaintStream tribes are creator communities organized by discipline, interest, project, or audience. LDSPMA tribes can become year-round communities where writers, musicians, filmmakers, educators, artists, voice actors, podcasters, and other creators continue building relationships long after an event ends.

This gives LDSPMA a practical way to support members between conferences rather than waiting for the next annual gathering.

Community functions
  • Discipline-specific feedback channels.
  • Project teams and collaboration spaces.
  • Mentor discussions and office hours.
  • Release announcements and member promotion.
LDSPMA contests and recognition

SaintStream can host LDSPMA-mentored and LDSPMA-judged contests. Winners, finalists, and notable submissions can be highlighted across the platform, giving creators meaningful recognition and giving LDSPMA a visible role in discovering talent.

5

Children's Media & Family Distribution

A contested battleground for children's hearts — and SaintStream's most meaningful opportunity.

See more

The battle for the hearts, minds, values, and beliefs of children is arguably the most contested space in modern society. SaintStream is being built to become a trusted, faith-centered counterweight — and to give every child free access to media that is child-safe, family-promoting, parent-respecting, and uplifting.

Books, music, podcasts, films, animation, educational content, audiobooks, and family entertainment can all live within one trusted ecosystem where families know the content aligns with their values.

Children's media is also one of the most practical entry points for college students in every creative field. A short illustrated story, a Primary-style song, a simple animation, a narrated scripture story, an educational video, or a faith-building audio episode becomes real portfolio work — reviewed by faculty, improved through mentorship, published on SaintStream, shared with families, funded by donors, and connected to LDSPMA.

From first project to professional

Over time, students move from small children's projects into books, films, albums, documentaries, animation series, podcasts, courses, and professional-level work. Children's media is not a side category — it is the beginning of a creator development pathway.

Finding the audience

Creating meaningful media is difficult. Funding it is difficult. Finding the audience is often hardest. SaintStream is designed to solve that final challenge by becoming a trusted destination where families actively come looking for uplifting content.

The complete loop
  • Students create.
  • Professors guide.
  • Mentors support.
  • Donors fund.
  • Families benefit.
  • Children receive free access.
  • Creators build portfolios.
  • LDSPMA gains early relationships with rising talent.

Creator Funding

Worthwhile projects need a path from idea to funding to audience.

See more

Many worthwhile projects never happen because creators cannot find funding.

SaintStream is being designed to support project-based fundraising for filmmakers, authors, animators, musicians, podcasters, educators, and student teams.

Project funding through tribes

Through their own tribe and project page, creators can publicize progress and invite financial backing from people who already care about the work: family, ward members, friends, neighbors, local supporters, and the broader SaintStream community.

  • Funding goal and progress.
  • Project description, trailer, sample, or proof of concept.
  • Milestones and updates.
  • Team members, mentors, and professor involvement.
  • Donor support options.
LDSPMA donor role

LDSPMA could also receive donor funds designated for worthy student projects, then help distribute those funds through a transparent project-review and mentorship process.

This gives students practical experience in presenting, marketing, and funding meaningful creative work.

Education & Mentorship

Students and creators can be mentored by LDSPMA members throughout the year.

See more

SaintStream can eventually host workshops, classes, conference recordings, mentorship resources, and student project reviews.

Experienced LDSPMA members can mentor students and emerging creators through the creative process, giving them guidance that is practical, personal, and connected to real projects.

Future educator tools
  • Faculty project review.
  • Assignment-based student submissions.
  • Mentor feedback from LDSPMA members.
  • Course and workshop libraries.
  • Student project showcases.
AI training as part of creator development

SaintStream's education layer can include practical, responsible training in AI-assisted creative workflows — writing, editing, research, storyboarding, audio, video, design, marketing, and project management.

The goal is to help faith-centered creators move faster while preserving originality, human judgment, and values-aligned content standards. AI is treated as a tool to amplify a creator's voice, not replace it.

The larger vision

When I step back, I see something larger than either organization alone. I see a system where LDSPMA provides professional trust, mentorship, and organizational support, while SaintStream provides the digital infrastructure and public-facing creator community.

In partnership with LDSPMA, SaintStream can become the place where university professors onboard student creators into a real creative ecosystem: students form teams, post project increments and final submissions, receive feedback, and build work that can serve families and children.

Students collaborate daily Professors guide projects LDSPMA mentors support Donors fund meaningful work Families discover uplifting media Children receive free access
Professor onboarding — how schools enter

LDSPMA can help onboard university professors who want their students to do more than complete disposable assignments.

  • Professors assign students to post drafts, increments, checkpoints, and final projects.
  • Students receive structured feedback from professors, peers, mentors, and creator communities.
  • Projects can be shaped toward real benefit for the SaintStream community of families and children.
Student creator teams — how collaboration works

Students form tribe teams around real projects, learning how to collaborate across disciplines and meet public-facing goals.

  • Writers, musicians, illustrators, filmmakers, editors, educators, and marketers work together.
  • Teams build portfolios and learn practical production habits.
  • Daily development replaces occasional inspiration.
LDSPMA communication — how the organization stays present

LDSPMA can communicate through SaintStream all year rather than only around conference season.

  • Announcements, contests, mentorship calls, award results, and member opportunities.
  • Discipline-specific tribes for feedback and collaboration.
  • Continuing connection between students, professionals, mentors, and audiences.
Funding meaningful work — how projects get resources

Creators can publicize project progress and invite support from people who already care: family, ward members, friends, neighbors, local supporters, donors, and the broader SaintStream community.

  • Student Creator Fund, Children’s Media Fund, Family Access Fund, and project-specific campaigns.
  • LDSPMA may receive donor funds for worthy student projects and distribute them through review and mentorship.
  • Students gain valuable marketing and fundraising experience.
Family distribution — how impact reaches homes

SaintStream gives families one trusted place to find uplifting, faith-centered media across formats.

  • Listen, Watch, Read, Learn, Aspire, and children’s content lanes.
  • Curated discovery instead of engagement-addiction algorithms.
  • Parent-respecting, family-promoting, child-safe media.
The complete loop — why it matters
  • Students collaborate.
  • Professors guide.
  • LDSPMA mentors support.
  • Donors fund.
  • Families benefit.
  • Children receive free access.
  • Creators build portfolios.
  • LDSPMA gains early relationships with rising talent.

SaintStream is not intended to replace what LDSPMA already does. My hope is that it can become digital infrastructure that helps LDSPMA do more of it.

What exists today at SaintStream.app is only the beginning, but I believe the long-term opportunity is significant.

I’d love your thoughts on how we might build something together that strengthens creators, serves families, develops future professionals, and expands the reach of faith-centered media for the next generation.

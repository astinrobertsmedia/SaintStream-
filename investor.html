The problem

Faith and family audiences don't have a trusted, modern home for media. Mainstream platforms mix in content that conflicts with their values and bury faith-aligned work. Existing "clean" options are fragmented and dated — and they serve listeners only, leaving faith-aligned creators with no real distribution home.

The solution — two front doors

For listeners

A clean, curated, family-safe catalog across lanes — Listen, Watch, Read, Aspire — with AI-assisted discovery and zero objectionable content.

For creators

A distribution home for faith-aligned artists (musicians, authors, narrators, filmmakers): community "Tribes," moderation, and direct audience relationships.

Why we're different

  • Curated & moderated — human + AI review, a two-person removal rule, family-safe by default. Trust is the product.
  • Creator-first — every artist gets a Tribe; fans unlock unreleased work; creators own the relationship.
  • Values-aligned business — no advertising, ever. No paid promotion, ever. Revenue comes from members, not attention.
  • Faith-aligned, not faith-themed — premium, modern design; none of the clichés.
